# Optional48 trajectory samples — bounded candidate

The actual four-host detail images are not visually accepted. Point kernels remain separate bright ellipsoids; V2 replaces them with twelve smooth elongated patches. Reducing the central fraction mainly removes the central/nose patch. In the saved `segment05` state the twelve root packets carry19 reference area, with mass .95–2.375 per representative. The real residual/show photographs already recorded in `PYRO-SMOKE-VISUAL-PHYSICS-REVIEW.md` have less regular connected lobes and broken edges; their exposure and time after the burst do not calibrate this model.

The candidate changes **only spatial source sampling**: `createPyroSmoke(...,{sourceModel:'trajectory-v1',kernelModel:'past-segment-v2',centralFraction:.05,trajectorySlots:48})`. Only12 or48 are accepted. Default12 preserves the frozen complete V2 adapter/host source after fourteen exact inverse edits, plus runtime point/V2 snapshots and source ledgers. Legacy9 refuses the48 option. The source ledger, raw light intensity, stored exposure, five-node segment moments, wind history and prescribed transport are unchanged.

The same deterministic pattern-stratified maximin selects up to48 actual roots, then partitions all real roots by their actual base-velocity distance. Each representative receives its count-weighted share, with the final residual assigned to preserve the total. No selection reads per-star colour or intensity. Each selected root still emits **once**, alternately at birth+.35 or+.65s. It does not emit at both times. An aerial event with at least48 roots therefore has9 launch+3 central+48 root=60 packets, not108 or120. Total reference area remains20.35. Fewer roots explicitly cap the chosen count; five actual roots produce17 packets with the same total area.

This addresses one demonstrated error source: twelve representatives concentrate the material from about120 actual roots into a few large optical peaks. More samples spread the same material over more real trajectories, so it may improve connectedness and reduce individual lump contrast. It does **not** create turbulence or guarantee natural edges. Keeping total mass does not preserve local peak density, transmittance or image brightness. V2 still collapses a short past segment to one Gaussian, loses future thermal cross covariance, uses prescribed constant diffusion, and lights each ray's packet contribution at its conditional optical mean. These limits remain after increasing sample count.
