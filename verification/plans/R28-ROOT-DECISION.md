## Scope and causal separation

Use the exact existing SH1 incoming-light model and the complete frozen native ledger: 755 density packets, 1,728 current emission rows and 1,304,640 logical pairs. These rows differ from SourceT's 1,734 optical rows. All 9,060 signed coefficients have an independent 256-node positive-Laplace offline reference; selected higher-node checks converge. No all-pair CPU reference belongs inside browser dispatch.

The numerical change is exact scheduling: retain 64-source inner sums, original packet/tile indexing, increasing-tile reduction, the original 1/(4pi) factor and three signed RGBA coefficient attachments. The fourth component is C1.z, not opacity. One complete native request is three global-index rectangles plus one full allocated final reduction; zero suffixes remain meaningful for the existing cold-zero reuse. Anisotropy .32 and the .002 kernel tolerance stay unchanged. This remains the trace-isotropic Gaussian average SH1 surrogate, without incoming source-path smoke shadow or full anisotropic radiative transport.

The independent baseline witness also demonstrates a real generation bug: success A, failed B after final-target mutation, then A can claim the old cache success without rebaking. The candidate retires the association and exposed field on failure; all map/final failure points and both serial/tiled return-to-A paths are tested. This is a cache correctness repair, not proof of a reported exposure symptom's cause.

## Why 1.4m is not the endpoint

The new current N8/contact lifetime study replays the exact R27 physical producer, terrain, current source provider and view extraction at 203 optical checkpoints. The +1.18s data matches R27 exactly. Sampled later maxima are SourceT 2,243,416 and SH 3,219,099; sampled combined demand peaks at 5,157,272 across two different kernels. This is CPU work-count evidence, not a continuous maximum, shared-cost estimate, GPU timing or multi-firework test. Historical N10/no-contact 3.986m remains a distinct fixture.

Therefore a midphase GPU pass is only the next causal step. It does not settle the known later single-firework cutoff. After the full SH route is verified, measure actual combined normal-frame cost and current later phases before calibrating a larger exact request budget or considering bounded optical acceleration. Retain all smoke mass, source IDs/colors/order and physical lifetimes. Continuous accumulated smoke, full evolving frames and final visual quality still require direct acceptance.
